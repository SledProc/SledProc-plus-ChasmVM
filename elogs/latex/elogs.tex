\shead{Machinery Space Operations}

\vspace{3em}
\sshead{.55\textwidth}{Create}

\vspace{2em}


\begin{enumerate}

\item{}  From the Electronic Record Books (\eLogs{}) screen (ship level), select the 
\empht{Oil Record Book Part I -- Machinery Space Operations}.

\item{}  On the record book menu, select the \empht{Create} button. 
The record book's screen displays.

\item{}  Select the appropriate \empht{Record Date} and \empht{Category}. 
The form will populate with additional sections for data entry.

\vspace*{14pt}

\hspace*{-60pt}\includegraphics[scale=.55,
 trim=0 0 0 130pt, clip]{pics/pics/49.png}

\vspace*{17pt}

\Tip{} \eLogs{} uses Greenwich Mean Time (\textbf{GMT}) and 
Local Time (\textbf{LT}) in record detail. The time is
entered using military time format. Time entries display in 
the \empht{Preview Record Book Entry}, in
the exported file and can be found in the print out of the record.

\item{}  Enter the appropriate information in all required fields. Required fields are underlined
in \textcolor{red}{red}.

\clearpage{}

\item{}  \textbf{Optional}. For \empht{Tank Details}, there are 
three options that can be selected where ballasting
was performed: \empht{Already Cleaned}, \empht{Not Cleaned}, \empht{Performed Cleaning}.

\vspace*{14pt}

\hspace*{-60pt}\includegraphics[scale=.85,
 trim=0 0 0 130pt, clip]{pics/pics/50.png}

\vspace*{14pt}

%\clearpage{}

\textbullet{} To view the \empht{Vessel's Last Known Status}, 
click the button located to the top right of the screen
just above the \empht{Save As Draft} button. The \empht{Vessel's Last Known Status} 
dialog will be opened.  Click either \empht{Vessel's Operational Status} 
or \empht{Engine Status} for details.

\vspace*{9pt}


\Tip{} This feature can be used on a rig or offshore vessel to view the time and location of
the vessel when the logbook is accessed.

\sshead{.55\textwidth}{Attachments and signature}

\vspace{2em}

\item{}  For all \textbf{Categories}: to \textbf{add attachments}, click the browse to upload hyperlink located in the
\empht{Attachments} section and select the appropriate file. File types that can be attached
include \bfs{PDF}, \bfs{DOCX}, \bfs{XLSX}, \bfs{PNG}, \bfs{JPG}, \bfs{GIF}, and \bfs{BMP}.

%\vspace*{14pt}

\item{}  \textbf{Optional}. To save the record book entry as a draft 
rather than submitting it for approval, in the \empht{Preview Record Book Entry} 
section, click the \empht{Save as Draft} button.  Once a record is saved as a 
draft, the \empht{Delete} button will appear in the
\empht{Preview Record Book Entry} section.

\vspace*{25pt}

\hspace*{-54pt}\includegraphics[scale=1,
 trim=0 0 0 70pt, clip]{pics/pics/ORB-A.png}

\vspace*{14pt}

\item{}  In the \empht{Sign} section, enter the 
\empht{Officer-In-Charge}, \empht{Rank}, and signature.

\item{}  From the \empht{Preview Record Book Entry} section, click the 
\empht{Submit} button. An \empht{Enter Your PIN Number} dialog displays.

\item{}  Enter the appropriate \textbf{Pin Number} and then click \qce{Ok}.

\end{enumerate}
