\textit{In blessed memory of Jeremiah B. McKenna---Chief Counsel to
the New York State Senate Committee on Crime and Correction 
and Executive Director of the Policy Sciences Center at Yale Law School---the
first champion of the protective mother and the first legislative counsel to
write an extensive memorandum of law on the injustices of the family
courts and on the corrupt practices of child welfare agencies in a
Brooklyn Orthodox Jewish case that would later become a national cause
célèbre, and be profiled in The Jewish Standard, The Jewish Press, The
Jewish Link, and in Jewish Life Magazine. We also dedicate this book to
protective mothers across the nation and to the tireless advocates that
support them.}
